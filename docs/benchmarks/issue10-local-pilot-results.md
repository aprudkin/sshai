**Checkpoint:** 2026-10-03, after the first independent model assessment.

**Scope:** the original four-slot local pilot under the
[prospective methodology amendment](issue10-methodology-amendment.md).

This is a pilot report, not a completed experiment, confirmatory token-effect estimate,
README savings figure or claim of quality non-degradation. It retains two startup
failures and one completed task pair. No failed slot was retried or replaced.

## Technical corrections and evidence boundaries

- The first failure exposed native Codex temporary helper directories that the initial
  inventory check did not recognize. The correction recognizes only the pinned layout.
- The second attempt reported a revoked refresh token. Supported browser login restored
  the dedicated credential source. The failed attempt was retained; login success was
  not treated as proof that a subsequent model request would succeed.
- Recorded `world_state` context metadata and the `error` notification preceding a
  separate `turn.failed` required prospective capture-adapter corrections. Historical
  reports, including their original parser issues, were not rewritten.
- Named no-model canaries passed for M01 and M02 access restrictions. They test specified
  allowed/denied accesses, not complete OS confinement. The later M01 sessions preserved
  fixture hashes and produced bounded captures without native warning/error/reroute
  notices, compaction or capture overflow.

For each completed diagnostic capture, outside-assessment qualification checked the
observed pinned-version producer semantics: one matching thread/turn, a synchronous
`final_answer` `AgentMessage` with one `Text` block, successful terminal completion,
matching finalized CLI/file text, no post-terminal records and normal process exit.
Independent review accepted those two specific qualifications and the bounded routing
and cumulative-usage evidence. This was not an inference from hashes or answer
correctness alone. Original automatic completion/delivery fields remain unknown and
historical coordinator eligibility flags remain false; the qualifications are separate
append-only evidence.

Within the recorded ResponseItem request domain, baseline made two fixture-read
requests. The sshai arm made four exact-pinned invocations, including an empty-body
usage error and auxiliary `pwd`, followed by an allowed ordinary read of its saved
artifact. All model-visible steps, including that error and artifact retrieval, remain
in diagnostic usage. There was no observed raw source-fixture bypass in the sshai arm.
These request counts are not cross-source unique-call counts or OS-execution attestations.

## Independent model assessment

One fresh assessor context received both unchanged M01 finals, the neutral task,
original fixtures, source-bound semantic key and frozen rubric. Answer IDs/order were
randomized. Owner mappings, arm labels, trial order, diagnostic usage and prior grades
were not supplied as assessment metadata. Unchanged answer wording can still reveal
an arm, so lexical blinding is incomplete.

The separately reviewed one-context runner used the fixed model/effort, disabled model
tools, qualified named packet/owner/control access restrictions and retained a one-shot
reservation. The actual context completed without recorded tool calls or native
warning/error/reroute notices. Main separately checked its final-phase/terminal/delivery
semantics; this new capture was not implicitly covered by the earlier independent
review of diagnostic captures. The original runner finality/quality fields were not
promoted. Bridge validation accepted both returned assessments and their source-line
coordinates. Both received correct diagnosis, evidence 2 and recommendation 2, with
no returned disputes, unsupported-statement or unsafe-recommendation entries.

These are **model assessments**, not human grades, objective truth or independent human
calibration. Coordinate checks do not establish quotation accuracy or causal relevance.
M02's private packet retains both missing-answer outcomes with zero eligible answers;
no model context was launched to invent their scores, and no replacement is allocated.

## Readiness decision and remaining work

The two successful contexts and separate assessment establish usable local evidence
paths for the observed version/configuration. The M02 startup failures leave its live
large-case capture and assessment behavior untested in this pilot; synthetic checks
and access canaries do not fill that gap. Qualification does not extend to unobserved
record shapes, compaction, other platforms or hidden backend changes.

Proceed with preparation of the already-prescribed local measured allocation: six
tasks, three paired repetitions each, 36 diagnostic slots. Any launch still requires
its frozen prospective manifest, reviewed implementation and fresh access readiness.
Advancement is based on the technical evidence path, **not favorable token or quality
outcomes**. Every later slot must retain unsupported/capture failures; there are no
replacement sessions, reduced denominators or outcome-based stopping rules.

The local measured series and both remote series have not run. Linux and Windows need
their own target, shell, fixture and access qualification; a local result does not
qualify them. The study ceilings remain 120 diagnostic slots and 24 assessment contexts.
At this checkpoint, four diagnostic slots and one assessment context are consumed.
No token-savings or whole-issue completion claim is justified.

Raw streams, answers, keys, credentials and private target/path details remain outside
the repository. Retention follows the amendment's 90-day policy without automatic deletion.
