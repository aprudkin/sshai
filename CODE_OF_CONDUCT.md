# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes respect, constructive feedback, collaboration, and empathy. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository owner through the contact route on the GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or take other action appropriate to the circumstances.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
